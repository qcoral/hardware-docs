## Overview

Once you've finished your hardware and have _finally_ gotten it approved, you'll get an HCB card grant in your email!

![image](https://cdn.hackclub.com/01a01b8d-69e6-7837-b24d-7f083183a316/paste-1787168647284.png)
_it'll look something like this on [hcb.hackclub.com](https://hcb.hackclub.com)!_

This is a virtual credit card with some amount of money on it, depending on your project!

It's an international VISA card, so make sure your vendor accepts international cards before ordering!

_for those curious: internally we use stripe as a provider!_

## Usage

![image](https://cdn.hackclub.com/01a1d102-f6fa-7b46-907f-f45c0c7ba0f5/paste-1794507994258.png)

**To use it**, punch in the details you get when you open the card! Make sure to use the billing address displayed on the card, not your own. It may not match the image above!

After making a purchase, **you MUST upload your receipts on HCB!** This ensures we can keep our nonprofit status.

**Do not:**

- Use the card to enable free trials. This will get us banned from Stripe and make us lose our ability to send out grants in the future!
- Buy extra items that you weren't approved for.

All unused funds go back to Hack Club HQ, so DO NOT unnecessarily spend money! We are a non profit and every dollar saved goes towards the next program.
